Privacy

This gallery uses no cookies, analytics or tracking, and loads nothing from other sites.

Who runs this site

Tigy, in Switzerland. Write to tigythegaymer@hotmail.com with any question about this page, or to ask what is kept about you or to have it deleted.

What the hosting records

The site runs on Google's Firebase App Hosting. For each request, Google's servers log your IP address, your browser, the page you asked for, the page you came from, the response and the time. These logs are kept for 30 days to keep the site running and fix errors, then deleted. Google keeps them as the host, and they may be stored outside Switzerland, including in the US, under Google Cloud's data processing terms. They aren't used to profile or follow anyone.

What your browser keeps

A few of your choices are kept in your browser so the site remembers them, and they never leave it. Your display settings, such as the theme and what the gallery shows, are kept until you change them back. Where you were in the gallery, and the pieces you chose to see, are kept until you close the tab.

You can refuse them by blocking site data in your browser's settings: the site still works, it just forgets your choices. You can also clear them there at any time.
